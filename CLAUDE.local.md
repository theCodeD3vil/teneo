### Shell (zsh)

_~1,800 tokens/session saved_

- Quote glob arguments in grep/find, e.g. `--include='*.d.ts'` and `--exclude-dir=.cache`. Unquoted globs made zsh abort with `no matches found` and forced a retry.
- `timeout` is often not found in this zsh. Wrap long commands with `perl -e 'alarm 90; exec @ARGV' <cmd>` instead, which worked in later runs.
- `grep` may resolve to ugrep, which prints ANSI codes and warnings. Use `/usr/bin/grep` or `rtk grep` for clean output.
